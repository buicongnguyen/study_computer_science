Practical notes for your daughter

These are the highest-signal habits for surviving the first year without getting surprised by avoidable issues.

Watch notices early

Korea University course registration details and seminar instructions change by semester, so department notices matter almost as much as the curriculum table.

Protect semester 1 foundations

If Computer Programming I or Mathematics for Computer Science I becomes shaky, fix that quickly because later core CS courses stack on top of them.

Treat low-credit required courses seriously

Seminar courses and online tasks are easy to underestimate, but they still affect required progress and can create needless stress if ignored.
